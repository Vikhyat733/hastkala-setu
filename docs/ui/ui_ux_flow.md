# MELA UI/UX Design System & Flow

## Core User Persona
**Rural Artisan (e.g. Ramesh, bell-metal caster from Bastar)**:
- Primary language: Hindi, Chhattisgarhi, Bengali, or Marathi.
- Digital literacy: Moderate to low; high familiarity with voice notes and camera interfaces.
- Pain points: Writing complex product descriptions in English, calculating competitive prices, and managing invoices.

## Screen Flow Hierarchy

```
[Splash Screen] 
       │
       ▼
[Language Selection] (Visual icons + native audio greeting)
       │
       ▼
[Onboarding] (3 visual cards explaining voice cataloging & direct earnings)
       │
       ▼
[Mobile OTP Auth] (Simple 4-digit OTP)
       │
       ▼
[Artisan Dashboard]
       ├── [Craft Camera & AI Enhancement] (Capture craft photo -> clean background)
       ├── [Voice Catalog Generator] (Speak in vernacular -> AI generates title & story)
       ├── [Smart Pricing Calculator] (Raw materials + hours worked -> fair price)
       ├── [Marketplace & B2B Inquiries] (Wholesale buyers & customer inquiries)
       ├── [Order Management] (Visual order cards with packing audio guides)
       ├── [Earnings & UPI Direct Payout] (Transparent breakdown, no middlemen cuts)
       └── [Virtual Business Assistant] (Audio/chat bot answering business queries)
```
