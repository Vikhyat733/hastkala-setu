# Smart India Hackathon 2026: Problem Statement SIH26090

## Overview
- **Problem Statement ID**: SIH26090
- **Theme**: AI-powered mobile marketplace and virtual business manager for marginalized rural artisans.
- **Context**: Across India, millions of traditional artisans produce exquisite crafts, handlooms, and cultural artifacts, but remain economically vulnerable due to middlemen, lack of digital cataloging tools, and geographic isolation.

## Core Mandates
1. **Vernacular Accessibility**: Zero language barrier through voice-driven interactions.
2. **AI-Driven Catalog Generation**: Converting raw photographs taken with budget smartphones into studio-grade marketplace listings.
3. **Fair Pricing Intelligence**: Algorithmic price recommendations accounting for labor, materials, and artisan heritage value.
4. **Virtual Business Management**: Automated order, inventory, and payment tracking with direct UPI settlements.
5. **Decoupled, Production-Ready Architecture**: Modular microservice and client architecture designed for scale and enterprise maintainability.
